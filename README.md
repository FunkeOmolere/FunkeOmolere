# Funke Omolere

🏆 One of the Most Inspiring Women in Cyber 2026
🎤 Speaker — BSides London 2025
GRC Lead at Adobe | GRC Engineer | Open Source Builder

---

I work at the intersection of governance, risk, compliance, and engineering. My day job is GRC Lead on the Marvel Team at Adobe. Outside of that, I build open source compliance tooling, write about GRC engineering, and share what I am learning in public.

I came into GRC through a non-traditional route and taught myself to build. I am a working mother of three doing school runs and git commits, and I think those two things are more compatible than people expect.

---

### What I am working on

**GRC Compliance Skills EMEA** — an open source Claude Skills library covering Cyber Essentials Plus, DORA, BSI C5, EU AI Act, TISAX, and Spain ENS. All 6 skills live at [funkeomolere.github.io/Claude-Skills-GRC-UK](https://funkeomolere.github.io/Claude-Skills-GRC-UK)

**ctrl.funke** — a personal GRC control tools dashboard covering ISO 27001, NIST CSF, DORA, EU AI Act, CE+, and more. Built for work use.

**Cybershift** — an AI-assisted compliance chatbot built on N8N, Pinecone, and OpenAI, currently covering NIST 800-53 with EMEA frameworks in progress.

**AI-assisted compliance monitoring** — exploring Claude Code SDK and Cursor for GRC automation.
